01 / CATALOG PROCESSINGAutomation engineering

An hour per product wasn't sustainable.

The value wasn't in an impressive script. It was in the time no longer spent repeating the same steps.

See where the story began ↗
Problem
Catalog preparation repeatedly consumed around 60 minutes per product.
Approach
Map the recurring tasks and translate the consistent parts into a repeatable automated workflow.
Evidence / impact
Processing reduced to under seven minutes per product; reported 88% faster. Daily volume reported at 200+ products.
Lesson
A useful automation starts with understanding the work, not selecting a technology.
02 / GENLOGIN FLOW EXPLORERInteractive systems

Complexity needed a map.

A node count isn't an outcome. But a navigable map can turn a hard-to-follow system into something people can question.

Problem
Multiple workflows and their many connections were difficult to inspect as one system.
Approach
Build an interactive node map designed for exploration rather than a static diagram.
Evidence / impact
19 workflows and 2,489 mapped nodes. The interactive GenLogin explorer exists in the current portfolio; usability outcomes still require measurement.
Lesson
Observability and explanation are part of the work of engineering.
03 / SKU CLASSIFICATIONData tooling

A code should be understandable.

When a catalog grows, consistency matters more than memorizing another identifier.

Problem
Product identifiers and variant information can become difficult to interpret at catalog scale.
Approach
Make SKU logic explorable through a lightweight decoder and explicit rules.
Evidence / impact
2,000+ classified products, 1,600+ variants, and an interactive SKU decoder. Accuracy rates and intervention reduction are not yet verified.
Lesson
Readable rules make systems easier to improve and check.
04 / EMAIL AUTOMATIONNode.js / walkthrough

Show me what happens after send.

A live walkthrough can explain a technical process more clearly than an architecture diagram alone.

Problem
People need to understand a multi-step email flow rather than seeing only the final message.
Approach
Present a Node.js email flow as a step-by-step technical walkthrough.
Evidence / impact
A live-build walkthrough is present in the current site. Delivery reliability, execution time, and production volume are not supplied.
Lesson
Make system behavior visible before you ask people to trust it.
