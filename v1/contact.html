Where I can help

A useful place to start.

A few kinds of problems fit the work in this portfolio. We can begin with the bottleneck rather than the tool.

  1. 01

    Automation for operations

    Repeated catalog tasks, handoffs, data checks, or workflows that ask people to do the same work twice.

  2. 02

    Applied AI tools

    Small AI-assisted systems where inputs, human review, and possible errors need to remain visible.

  3. 03

    Focused web systems

    Interfaces that help people inspect, operate, or understand a process—without unnecessary complexity.
