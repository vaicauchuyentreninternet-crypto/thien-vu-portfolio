ABOUT
I work best where operations and technology meet.

        

          
My role tends to start when a process has become too manual, fragmented or difficult to maintain. I like understanding what the team is actually doing before deciding what the system should be.

          
I learn quickly, prototype fast and stay close to the people who use the workflow. The goal is usually simple: fewer repeated steps, clearer information and a process that is easier to trust.

          
I use AI as one part of the toolkit, alongside automation, data structure and web development. I care more about whether the system works in daily operations than whether the technology sounds impressive.
